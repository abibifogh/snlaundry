# Abibifo Crafts & More — Logo Kit

*where tradition, passion and creativity converge*

A cleaned-up, fully vector version of the A/C logo, with ready-made copies for print, web, social media and email.

## What was polished

- **Rebuilt as vector art.** The A and C were traced from the original and smoothed into crisp outlines, so they stay sharp at any size, from a business card to a banner.
- **Removed the soft grey drop shadow and the glow behind the ring.** It looked muddy in print and on coloured backgrounds. The mark is now flat and clean.
- **Rebuilt the ring with a single even stroke and a clean opening.** A stray fragment of the ring that overlapped the C was removed.
- **Fixed the base rules.** The two lines under the ring were 4 px out of level and off-centre. They are now level and centred on the ring.
- **Fixed the wordmark spacing.** "ABIBIFO CRAFTS & MORE" ran almost to the edge of the canvas. It now has even margins and consistent letter-spacing.
- **Centred the tagline** under the wordmark (it was pushed to the right) and gave it a clear size relationship to the wordmark.
- **Converted all text to outlines**, so the logo looks the same on every computer without the fonts installed.

| **wordmark** | Where the mark is already shown nearby, or for very narrow spaces |

## Fonts (free, Google Fonts)

- Wordmark: **Jost Medium**, all caps, letter-spacing +200
- Tagline and body text: **Cormorant Garamond Bold** (use Regular or SemiBold for body copy)

## Usage rules

- **Clear space:** keep empty space around the logo at least the height of the "C".
- **Minimum size:** stacked logo 30 mm / 180 px wide; horizontal 45 mm / 240 px wide; monogram 8 mm / 32 px. Below those sizes, use the monogram.
- **Don't** stretch, rotate, recolour outside the versions above, add shadows or glows, or place the colour version on busy photos. Use the `white` version on photos instead.
